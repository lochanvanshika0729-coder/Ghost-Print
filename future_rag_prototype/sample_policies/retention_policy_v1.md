# Purpose and scope
ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE ONLY — fictional demonstration content, not a legal or regulatory requirement.

## Retention review
Classify each discovered record before recommending a change. A business, security, or legal hold may require a record to remain available for a defined period. The reviewer should identify the record category, retention basis, approving policy reference, start date, and end or review date when supplied. Missing retention metadata is a question for human review, not permission to erase a record or proof that it must be retained.

## Minimize retained information
Where retention is authorized, retain only the fields permitted by the applicable policy. Remove or transform direct identifiers when the policy calls for redaction or anonymization. Do not treat a stable user identifier as anonymous without evidence that it can no longer be linked to the individual.

## Evidence and expiry
Keep evidence of the observed record state and the source policy version used for the decision. After the retention period expires, create a new review and verify the record and any copies under the applicable deletion process. This illustrative sample does not set a real retention duration.

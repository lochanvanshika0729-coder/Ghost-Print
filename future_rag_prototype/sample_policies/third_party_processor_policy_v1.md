# Purpose and scope
ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE ONLY — fictional demonstration content, not a legal or regulatory requirement.

## Processor inventory
Identify processors and downstream providers that may hold a copy of the data. Use supplied contracts, processing records, evidence manifests, and service documentation to establish which organizations and systems are actually in scope. Do not assume a processor has a copy solely because it is connected to the organization.

## Deletion coordination
For each confirmed processor copy, determine the requested treatment, any documented retention exception, the request and response dates, and the evidence available to support the response. A processor's completion notice is a claim that should be assessed alongside evidence about the resource state and any active exports or synchronization jobs.

## Follow-up
When information is missing, identify the exact processor, resource, evidence gap, and follow-up check for a human reviewer. This sample policy cannot establish contractual obligations or legal compliance.
